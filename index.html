The LIATE Priority Rule

When choosing u for Integration by Parts, pick the function type that appears earliest in LIATE.

L
Logarithmic

ln(x), log_2(x)

I
Inverse Trig

arctan(x), arcsin(x)

A
Algebraic

x, x^2, x^3 + 1

T
Trigonometric

sin(x), cos(x), sec^2(x)

E
Exponential

e^x, 2^x, e^(3x)

Formula Reminder:
$$\int u \, dv = u v - \int v \, du$$

Choose u from the higher category in LIATE, and let dv be whatever remains (including dx).
